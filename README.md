# CPSC 349 Project - StudySync

StudySync is a responsive web application designed to help college and high school 
students efficiently organize their academic workload, track coursework, and balance 
their daily study habits. Between balancing multiple syllabi, varying assignment 
deadlines, and personal commitments, students frequently struggle with time 
management and prioritizing tasks effectively.

The platform solves this problem by providing a centralized academic hub where users 
can input enrolled courses, log assignments with specific due dates, and generate 
structured study sessions. By visualizing upcoming deadlines and tracking daily 
progress, students can eliminate last-minute cramming, reduce academic stress, and 
stay on top of their semester goals.

## Major Features:
1. **Course Management:** Add, edit, and organize individual courses with instructor 
details, schedules, and custom color tags. 
2. **Assignment & Deadline Tracking:** Create, categorize, and prioritize 
assignments, exams, and projects with associated due dates and completion 
statuses. 
3. **Interactive Study Calendar & Schedule:** View upcoming academic tasks and 
planned study blocks in a weekly or monthly interactive calendar view. 
4. **Study Session Planner & Timer:** Break large assignments into focused study 
sessions with an integrated timer (e.g., Pomodoro method) and checklist. 
5. **Progress & Completion Dashboard:** Visualize completed vs. pending tasks 
and track overall weekly study productivity. 

# Planned Pages/Screens:
